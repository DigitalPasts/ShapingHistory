**How this SI is organised.** Sections S1 to S6 document the corpus, the silhouettes and damage, period recognition and the museum layer, the Old Babylonian labels and regional lines, the tablet proportions, and the case studies with the VAE dimensions; they are carried over from the October draft with their numbers checked. Sections S7 to S12 are new: the dates from the specialist databases (S7), the turning-point protocol with its power analysis (S8), the blind change-point scan (S9), the polity scores and their relation to uniformity (S10), archives and collections (S11), and the results withdrawn or revised during the study (S12). S13 lists code, data and datasets. The Appendix is the full codebook of the polity scores. Every number comes from a numbered script in the code release (script numbers in brackets). Figure numbers in S1 to S6 still follow the October numbering and will be renumbered once the main figures are chosen (see the separate figure-options file).
