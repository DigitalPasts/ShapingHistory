## Structure (as in recent PNAS papers in archaeology and early writing)

Significance and Abstract, then an Introduction without a heading, then Results (four headed subsections), then Discussion (ending in a short Conclusions paragraph), then Materials and Methods, then Data availability, Acknowledgments and References. Previous literature stays inside the Introduction and in each Results subsection, where it is needed. Everything else goes to the SI.
